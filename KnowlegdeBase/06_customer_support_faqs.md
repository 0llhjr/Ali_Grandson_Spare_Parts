## Section A — Account & Registration

**Q: How do I create an account?**  
A: Open the Ali Grandsons app and tap **CREATE ACCOUNT** on the home screen. Fill in your username, full name, email address, phone number, password (minimum 8 characters), and date of birth. Tap CREATE ACCOUNT to submit. You will be redirected to the login screen.

**Q: I forgot my password. How can I reset it?**  
A: Contact us via WhatsApp at +968 9576 0754 or email aligrandsoncompany@gmail.com with your registered email address. We will reset your password and email you a new one.

**Q: Can I change my username after registration?**  
A: No. Your username is permanent and cannot be changed after registration. You may update all other profile details (name, email, phone, password) from your Profile (tap the person icon in the bottom navigation bar).

**Q: Can I use the same email for multiple accounts?**  
A: No. Each email address can only be linked to one account.

**Q: How do I update my profile information?**  
A: Tap the **Profile** icon (person icon) in the bottom navigation bar. Edit the relevant fields and tap SAVE CHANGES.

**Q: How do I change my password?**  
A: Go to your Profile (bottom navigation → Profile icon). Update the password field and tap SAVE CHANGES.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**  
A: To delete your account, contact our support team at aligrandsoncompany@gmail.com. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

**Q: I cannot log in — the app says invalid credentials.**  
A: Make sure you are using your **email address** (not username) to log in. Check that Caps Lock is not on. Ensure you have a working internet connection. If the problem persists, contact support to verify your account.

## Section B — Browsing & Searching Products

**Q: How do I search for a product?**  
A: Use the search bar in the maroon header at the top of the dashboard. Type a product name (e.g. "brake pad"), a brand (e.g. "Bosch"), or a car model (e.g. "Land Cruiser"). Results update as you type. Tap the filter icon (sliders) next to the search bar to open the advanced search & filter page.

**Q: How do I browse by category?**  
A: Below the promotional banners on the dashboard, there is a horizontal scrollable row of category circles (Engine Parts, Brake System, Filters, Electrical, Suspension & Steering, Body Parts, Transmission, Cooling System). Tap any circle to see all products in that category. Tap "See All" to view the full categories grid.

**Q: Can I filter products by vehicle type, brand, or price?**  
A: Yes. Tap the filter icon (sliders) next to the search bar to open the Search & Filter page. You can filter by: category, brand, vehicle type (Sedan, SUV, Pickup Truck, etc.), and price range, and toggle "In Stock Only".

**Q: How do I filter products by warehouse location?**  
A: Tap the warehouse selector at the very top of the dashboard header (shows the location name and a down-arrow). A sheet will appear listing available warehouses. Select one to see only products available at that location. Select "All Warehouses" to see all stock.

**Q: How do I know if a part fits my car?**  
A: Each product listing shows the Type, Brand, and Compatibility/Model field. If you are unsure, contact us on WhatsApp (+968 9576 0754) with your car's make, model, year, and VIN number.

**Q: A product I need is shown as "Out of Stock". What do I do?**  
A: Options: (1) Check back — stock is updated in real time. (2) Contact a branch directly. (3) You will receive an **automatic email notification** when the item is back in stock (this happens automatically when admin restocks it).

**Q: Can I see the product image before buying?**  
A: Yes. Each product has a photo. Tap any product card to open the full detail page with a large expanding hero image at the top.

**Q: Does the price include VAT or other taxes?**  
A: Yes. All displayed prices are final prices including all applicable taxes. There are no hidden fees.

## Section C — Cart & Wishlist

**Q: How do I add a product to my cart?**  
A: Open a product's detail page, tap **ADD TO CART** at the bottom. A dialog asks for the quantity you want. Enter a number (cannot exceed available stock) and tap ADD.

**Q: How do I view my cart?**  
A: Tap the **shopping bag icon** in the bottom navigation bar (or the gold badge in the top-right of the header). A gold badge shows how many different products are in your cart.

**Q: How do I remove an item from my cart?**  
A: In the cart screen, tap the red trash icon next to the item you want to remove.

**Q: How do I change the quantity of an item in my cart?**  
A: In the cart screen, use the + and − buttons next to each item. You cannot increase the quantity beyond what is in stock.

**Q: Is there a wishlist feature?**  
A: Yes. You can save products to your wishlist by tapping the **heart icon (♥)** on any product card or product detail page. Access your wishlist by tapping the heart icon in the bottom navigation bar. To remove a product from your wishlist, tap the filled red heart on the wishlist page or product detail page.

**Q: My cart is empty after I logged back in. What happened?**  
A: Your cart is linked to your account and should persist. If it appears empty, the items may have sold out. Please re-add them from the catalogue.

## Section D — Orders

**Q: How do I place an order?**  
A: Browse the catalogue, add items to your cart, tap the bag icon, then tap **PROCEED TO CHECKOUT**. Fill in your delivery address and choose your payment method (Cash on Delivery or Card). Tap **CONFIRM & PLACE ORDER**.

**Q: Where do I find my orders?**  
A: Tap the **My Orders** option from your profile area. The ACTIVE tab shows current orders (Pending, Ready, In Delivery). The COMPLETED tab shows past orders (Delivered and Cancelled).

**Q: How do I track my order?**  
A: Open My Orders in the app. The ACTIVE tab shows all current orders with real-time status (Pending → Ready → In Delivery → Delivered). You also receive email notifications for every status change.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**  
A: You can request cancellation while the order status is **PENDING** by contacting us via WhatsApp (+968 9576 0754) or email (aligrandsoncompany@gmail.com) with your order number. Orders that are READY or IN DELIVERY cannot be cancelled.

**Q: I placed an order but have not received a confirmation. What should I do?**  
A: Check your email inbox (including spam/junk folder) for a confirmation email from aligrandsoncompany@gmail.com. Also check My Orders in the app. If you still cannot find your order after 30 minutes, contact support.

**Q: Can I modify my order after placing it?**  
A: Orders cannot be modified after being placed. Cancel the order (if still PENDING) and place a new one with the correct items.

**Q: What does the order number look like?**  
A: Order numbers are displayed as #00001 through #99999 (5-digit zero-padded format). You will see it in My Orders and in all email notifications.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?**

## Section F — Payment

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**  
A: We accept:
1. **Cash on Delivery (COD)** — pay when the order arrives
2. **Credit/Debit Card** — Visa, Mastercard, American Express (in-app; real-time card validation)
3. **Bank Transfer** — for wholesale/corporate accounts only
4. **Branch payment** — cash or card when collecting at a branch

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**  
A: Yes. Card payments are processed through an encrypted PCI-DSS compliant gateway. We do not store your full card number.

**Q: The app shows my card number as invalid. Why?**  
A: The app performs a live Luhn algorithm check on your card number as you type. If the number turns red, it means the digits entered do not form a valid card number — check carefully for a mistyped digit. The checkout button is disabled until all card details are valid.

**Q: Can I pay part cash and part card?**  
A: No. Each order must use a single payment method.

## Section H — Technical Vehicle Questions

**Q: How often should I change my engine oil filter in Oman?**  
A: More frequently than European recommendations. In Oman's dusty climate, change your oil filter every **5,000 km** with conventional oil, or every **8,000–10,000 km** with full synthetic oil.

**Q: My car battery keeps dying in summer. Why?**  
A: Extreme heat (40–50°C in Oman's summer) accelerates battery degradation. Batteries typically last only **2–3 years** in Oman. Have your battery tested **free of charge** at any of our 7 branches.

**Q: How often should I change my wiper blades?**  
A: Every 12–18 months in normal use. In Salalah during the Khareef (monsoon), replace them before July. Desert sand degrades wiper rubber faster.

**Q: My air conditioner smells bad. What part do I need?**  
A: You likely need a new **Cabin Air Filter** (also called a Pollen Filter). In Oman replace every 15,000–20,000 km.

**Q: Which brake pads should I choose — ceramic or semi-metallic?**  
A: For everyday city driving in Muscat: **Ceramic brake pads** — quieter, cleaner, less dust. For heavy SUVs and off-road use: **Semi-metallic** — better heat resistance.
