# Knowledge Base — Document 09: App User Guide (Step-by-Step Instructions)

This document explains every screen and feature in the Ali Grandsons mobile app from the customer's perspective.

---

## Getting Started

### Installing the App

- Search **"Ali Grandsons"** on Google Play Store (Android) or Apple App Store (iOS).
- Download and install the app (free download).
- Open the app after installation.

### First Launch

When the app starts for the first time you will see the **Home screen** showing:
- The Ali Grandson logo in the centre (with a maroon glow shadow)
- "ALI GRANDSONS" and "Premium Spare Parts" text
- Three buttons: **CUSTOMER LOGIN**, **CREATE ACCOUNT**, **ADMIN ACCESS** (small, at the bottom)

If you have already logged in previously, the app will take you directly to your dashboard without showing the home screen.

---

## Creating a New Customer Account

1. Tap **CREATE ACCOUNT** on the home screen.
2. Fill in all required fields:

| Field | What to Enter |
|-------|--------------|
| Username | A unique ID you choose (e.g. ahmed_92). **Cannot be changed later.** |
| Full Name | Your real first and last name |
| Email Address | A valid email (used for login and order notifications) |
| Phone Number | Your Omani mobile number (e.g. +968 9123 4567) |
| Password | Minimum 8 characters |
| Date of Birth | Tap the field to pick from a calendar |

3. Tap **CREATE ACCOUNT**.
4. If the username or email is already taken, you will be notified — choose a different one.
5. On success you are taken to the Login screen. Log in with your email and password.

---

## Logging In

### Customer Login
1. Tap **CUSTOMER LOGIN** on the home screen.
2. Enter your registered **email address** (not username) and password.
3. Tap **SIGN IN**.
4. If correct, you go directly to the Customer Dashboard.
5. If incorrect, "Invalid email or password" appears.

### Admin Login
1. Tap **ADMIN ACCESS** (small grey text at the bottom of the home screen).
2. Enter Admin ID and password on the dark-themed admin login screen.
3. Tap **AUTHENTICATE**.
4. Default credentials: ID = `admin`, Password = `admin123`

### Staying Logged In
The app remembers your login. When you close and reopen the app, you go straight to your dashboard — no need to log in every time.

## Customer Dashboard (Main Screen)

The dashboard is divided into a **fixed header** at the top and a **scrollable content area** below.

### Fixed Maroon Header

The header stays visible at all times and contains:

| Element | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Warehouse selector** (top row) | Shows the currently selected warehouse (e.g. "All Warehouses"). Tap to switch warehouse and filter stock by location. |
| **Greeting** ("Hello, [Name]") | Shows your display name from your profile. |
| **Cart badge** (top right) | Shopping bag icon with a gold dot showing how many different products are in your cart. Tap to open your cart. |
| **Search bar** | Type any keyword to search the product catalogue. Results update as you type. Tap the **X** to clear the search. |
| **Filter button** (sliders icon) | Opens the advanced Search & Filter page. |

### Scrollable Content

After the header, scrolling down reveals:

1. **Special Offers banner carousel** — 3 promotional images that auto-advance every 5 seconds. Swipe manually. Small animated dots show the current banner.
2. **Category row** — Horizontal scrollable circles for each of the 8 product categories. Tap any circle to browse that category. Tap "See All" to open the full categories grid.
3. **All Products list** — Every product available (filtered by the selected warehouse). Shows: brand (gold text), product name, compatible model, price in OMR, and IN STOCK / OUT OF STOCK badge.

### Pull to Refresh
Pull down on the content area to reload products, banners, and cart count with fresh data.

---

## Warehouse Selector

The warehouse selector controls which location's stock you see.

1. Tap the location bar at the very top of the header (it shows the current warehouse name and a ▼ arrow).
2. A bottom sheet appears listing all available warehouses plus "All Warehouses".
3. Tap a warehouse to filter products to only show what's in stock there.
4. Tap "All Warehouses" to see combined stock.
5. Your selection is saved automatically so it persists when you close and reopen the app.

---

## Product Cards (Dashboard List)

Each product card in the list shows:
- **Product image** (left) — with a heart icon overlay for wishlist
- **Brand name** (gold, small uppercase text)
- **Product name** (large bold)
- **Compatible model/year**
- **Price in OMR**
- **IN STOCK** (green) or **OUT OF STOCK** (red) badge

Tap any card to open the full product detail page.

**Heart icon overlay:** Each product card has a small heart (♥) in the top-right corner of the image. Tap it to add or remove the product from your wishlist without opening the detail page.

---

## Search & Filter Page

Tap the **filter icon** (sliders) next to the search bar to open the advanced filter page.

Available filters:
- **Search bar** — keyword search across name, description, and brand
- **Category chips** — filter by one of the 8 app categories
- **Brand chips** — filter by brand name (Toyota, Bosch, NGK, etc.)
- **Vehicle Type chips** — filter by Sedan, SUV, Pickup Truck, Universal, etc.
- **Price range slider** — set minimum and maximum price in OMR
- **In Stock Only switch** — hide out-of-stock products

Results update automatically as you change any filter. Tap **Reset** in the top-right to clear all filters at once.

---

## Categories Page

Tap "See All" next to the Category section header to open the full categories grid.

- Shows all 8 categories as cards in a 2-column grid.
- Each card shows: category icon, category name, and a gold badge with the number of products in it.
- Tap any category card to open the **Category Products Page** for that category.

### Category Products Page

- Lists all products within the selected category.
- Has its own search bar and filter chips (In Stock, Brand, Vehicle Type) for narrowing results within the category.
- Pull to refresh.
- Tap any product to open the detail page.

---

## Product Detail Page

Tapping a product card opens a full-screen detail view with:
- **Expanding hero image** at the top (the app bar scrolls away and the image fills the screen; scroll down to see details)
- **Brand name** (gold text)
- **Product name** in large bold text
- **Price** in OMR (right side)
- **Availability badge** — "In Stock (X units)" in green, or "Out of Stock" in red
- **Full description** and part details
- **Specifications** — Type, Compatibility (model/year), Part ID

### Wishlist button (heart icon in app bar)
Tap the heart circle button in the top-right of the expanding app bar to add/remove from your wishlist. The icon fills in gold when wishlisted.

### Bottom Action Bar (pinned at bottom)
- **Heart button** (left) — another way to toggle wishlist
- **ADD TO CART** button (right, full width) — disabled and grey when out of stock

### Adding to Cart from the Detail Page
1. Tap **ADD TO CART**.
2. A dialog appears: "How many units would you like to add?"
3. Enter a quantity. If you enter more than available stock, you will be warned.
4. Tap **ADD** — the item is added and you are returned to the product list.

## Shopping Cart

### Access
Tap the **shopping bag icon** in the dashboard header (or in the bottom navigation bar).

### What You See
- Each item: product image, name, price per unit, and quantity
- **− / +** buttons to adjust quantity (capped by stock)
- **Trash icon** to remove the item
- Running total at the bottom

### Checkout
At the bottom: total amount in OMR + **PROCEED TO CHECKOUT** button.

## Checkout / Placing an Order

### Delivery Information Section
Fill in:
1. **Full Delivery Address** — building, street, area, city (be specific)
2. **Contact Phone Number** — pre-filled from your profile
3. **Special Instructions** — optional (e.g. "Ring doorbell", "Leave with guard")

### Payment Method Section
Choose one:
- **Cash on Delivery** — pay in cash when the order arrives
- **Card** — enter card holder name, card number (auto-formatted in groups of 4), expiry date (MM/YY), and CVV
  - The app detects your card type live (Visa / Mastercard / Amex / Discover) and shows a branded badge
  - The card number turns red if it fails the Luhn algorithm check
  - The expiry field turns red if your card is expired
  - The **CONFIRM & PLACE ORDER** button is disabled until all card details are valid

### Order Summary Section
Shows: Items Total, Delivery Fee: **FREE**, Grand Total in gold text.

### Placing the Order
Tap **CONFIRM & PLACE ORDER**. Wait briefly. A success message appears and you are taken back to the dashboard. Check My Orders for your new order.

## Support Center (AI Chatbot)

**Access:** Tap the **Chat** icon in the bottom navigation bar.

### How It Works
1. The screen opens with a greeting from the Ali Grandson AI assistant.
2. Type your question in the text field at the bottom and tap the **send** button.
3. The bot shows a "typing..." animation (three animated dots) while it processes.
4. The bot's response appears as a white bubble on the left (rendered as Markdown — supports bold, lists, tables, headings). Your messages appear as maroon bubbles on the right.

### FAQ Shortcut
Tap the **FAQ list icon** (in the app bar or next to the text input) to open a scrollable sheet of common questions. Tap any question to send it automatically.

### New Chat
Tap the **New Chat icon** (add chat bubble icon) in the app bar to clear the conversation and start fresh. This also resets the server-side session so the bot has no memory of the previous conversation.

### What the Chatbot Can Do
- Answer general questions (store hours, delivery, payment, returns, warranty) from its knowledge base
- Check **live stock and prices** by querying the database in real time
- Show **your order history and status** (securely scoped to your account)
- Check **your cart contents** and **your wishlist**
- Answer in Markdown format (tables, bullet lists, bold text)

### What You Can Change

## Troubleshooting Common App Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|---------|
| App not loading / blank screen | Check internet connection. Close and reopen the app. If persistent, reinstall. |
| Cannot log in | Use your **email** (not username) to log in. Check Caps Lock. |
| Product images not loading | Slow connection — wait a few seconds or pull to refresh. |
| Cart items disappeared | Items may have sold out. Re-add them from the catalogue. |
| Notification email not received | Check spam/junk folder. Verify email in Profile Settings. |
| App is running slowly | Close other apps. Ensure at least 100 MB of free storage. Update the app. |
| Card payment button stays grey | All card fields must be filled and valid (card number passes Luhn check, expiry is not in the past, CVV is 3–4 digits). |
